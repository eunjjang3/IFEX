# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

IFEX contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback, and make participation welcoming regardless of background or experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Project spaces

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and other spaces used to represent or coordinate the project.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports or personal information in an issue. Open an issue containing no incident details and ask a maintainer to establish a private contact channel. Security vulnerabilities must instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions are made on a best-effort basis with attention to context, safety, and proportionality.
