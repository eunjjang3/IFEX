IFEX Third-Party Notices

Lensfun camera database

The camera maker/model aliases and crop factors bundled with IFEX are an
adapted subset of the Lensfun database:
https://github.com/lensfun/lensfun/tree/master/data/db

Changes: lens and calibration records were omitted; camera records were
reduced to maker/model aliases and crop factor, sorted, and serialized as JSON
for local browser lookup. The exact upstream revision is recorded in the
generated dataset.
